NORTHLINE ANALYTICS — OPERATIONS GUIDE (2026)

This guide explains how Northline teams purchase goods, travel, support customers, and keep operational records. When in doubt, record the decision in the appropriate system and ask the functional owner listed below.

PURCHASING
For purchases below $500, submit a purchase request in the Finance Portal and obtain manager approval before ordering. Purchases of $500 or more also require Finance approval. Purchases above $5,000 require two competitive vendor quotes unless Finance grants a written exception. Split orders to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited. Attach the invoice and approval to the purchase record.

TRAVEL
Get manager approval before booking business travel. Use the preferred travel booking service where practical. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. For flights of six hours or more, premium economy may be approved by a department head. Submit travel expenses within thirty days after returning. Include receipts and the business purpose.

MEETINGS AND RECORDS
Meeting owners should share an agenda for decision-making meetings and record decisions and assigned owners within two business days. Store official project records in the team workspace, not personal drives. Retain contracts and signed vendor agreements in the Legal repository. Never delete a record subject to a legal hold.

FACILITIES
Visitors must be signed in and escorted in nonpublic areas. Report building access card loss to Facilities immediately. Emergency exits must remain clear. For a building emergency, follow posted evacuation instructions and call local emergency services when necessary.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
Log customer issues in the Support Desk on the day received. Acknowledge a standard customer request within one business day. Escalate suspected safety, privacy, or security incidents immediately to the incident response channel. Do not promise a resolution date unless the responsible team has confirmed it.

APPROVALS
Approvals must be recorded in the appropriate system or in a retained written message. Verbal approval alone is not sufficient for purchasing, travel, or access requests. If the guide does not specify a process, ask the relevant functional owner before proceeding.

VENDOR MANAGEMENT
Preferred vendors are listed in the Finance Portal. New vendors require Finance and Legal review before first purchase above $1,000. Do not auto-renew contracts without a recorded owner review thirty days before expiry.

PROJECT DELIVERY
Project charters document scope, milestones, and executive sponsor. Status updates are due every two weeks for active projects. Scope changes above ten percent effort require sponsor written approval.

BUSINESS CONTINUITY
If offices are unavailable, teams follow the remote work rules in the Employee Handbook and move stand-ups to the approved video platform. Critical customer services maintain on-call rotations published in the Operations calendar.

QUALITY AND SAFETY
Report workplace safety hazards to Facilities the same day. Customer-facing teams log product defects in the Support Desk with severity labels. Severity-one defects trigger the incident bridge within one hour during business hours.
