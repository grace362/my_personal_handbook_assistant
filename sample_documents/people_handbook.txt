NORTHLINE ANALYTICS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026)

Welcome to Northline Analytics. This handbook describes how we work together, take time off, get paid, and treat each other. If a rule is not written here, ask People Operations before you assume it.

TIME OFF AND LEAVE
Employees request planned vacation through the People Portal at least ten calendar days before the first day away. The manager approves or declines the request in the portal. Employees should not book nonrefundable travel until approval is recorded. For an unexpected illness, notify your manager before your scheduled start time and record the sick day in the People Portal when you are able. The company provides ten paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not roll over.

PARENTAL LEAVE
After six months of employment, a new parent may take twelve weeks of paid parental leave. The employee should contact People Operations at least thirty days before the expected leave when practical. People Operations coordinates the leave plan and benefits continuation. This policy applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement.

WORKING HOURS
The standard team collaboration hours are 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time, Monday through Friday. Teams may agree on different schedules with manager approval. Record working hours accurately; do not record time for another employee.

EXPENSES
Submit business expense reports in the Finance Portal within thirty days of purchase. Attach an itemized receipt for each expense over $25. A manager must approve travel and lodging before booking. The standard meal reimbursement limit is $60 per day while traveling for approved company business. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Personal commuting costs are not reimbursable.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager agreement. Keep your calendar current and protect confidential information when working in public. Use the company VPN on untrusted networks. Contact IT if your home office equipment needs accommodation.

PAY AND BENEFITS
Payroll is deposited on the fifteenth and final business day of each month. Direct deposit changes must be submitted in the employee portal at least five business days before payday. Benefits enrollment is completed within thirty days of a new hire's start date or a qualifying life event. Contact People Operations for plan documents.

CONDUCT AND SUPPORT
Treat coworkers, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, and threats are prohibited. Report a concern to your manager, People Operations, or the confidential reporting line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited. If you are unsure whether this handbook covers a situation, ask People Operations; do not assume a rule that is not written here.

PROBATION AND PERFORMANCE
New hires complete a ninety-day probation period. Managers hold a check-in at thirty and sixty days. Annual performance reviews occur each February. Compensation adjustments follow review outcomes and budget approval; there is no automatic cost-of-living increase unless announced by executive leadership.

LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
Each employee receives an annual learning stipend of $750 for approved courses, conferences, or certifications. Submit requests through the Learning Portal before registering. Certificates must be uploaded within thirty days of completion.

HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
Northline observes ten paid company holidays per calendar year, published in the People Portal each November. Holiday pay applies to eligible full-time employees. Contractors should refer to their statements of work.

EQUAL OPPORTUNITY
Northline Analytics is an equal opportunity employer. Hiring and promotion decisions are based on qualifications, experience, and business needs without regard to protected characteristics under applicable law.
